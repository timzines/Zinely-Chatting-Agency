Trial week questions

Quick answers.

Something else? Ask us ↗

Why isn't the trial free?

Because real chatters work real shifts on your account for seven days. You only pay our percentage on the tips and PPV they close, so if they don't sell, the week costs you nothing.

Do I have to hand over my password?

Not to ask, and not for the free estimate. Before day 1 we agree access using your platform's own options, in writing, including who approves changes. When the trial ends, that access is removed. How access works →

What happens if it doesn't work for me?

You say stop. We remove our access, send one invoice for the week's closed sales, and that's it. No notice period, no exit fee.

Can an agency trial one creator first?

Yes. Pick one account from your roster that meets the 9–10 subscribers a day rule. We run it under your brand, and you compare the week against what your own team did before. Chatting for agencies →

Does it work for AI models?

Yes, on Fanvue. Send the character guide and we chat in that voice; every message is still sent by a human. Fanvue's AI-content label stays on, and you stay responsible for the account. Fanvue chatting for AI models →
