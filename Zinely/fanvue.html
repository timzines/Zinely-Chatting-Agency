Fanvue chatting, answered

Quick answers.

Still curious? Talk to us ↗

Do you chat for AI models on Fanvue?

Yes. Most of our Fanvue work is for AI-model operators who already have traffic coming in. We work from your approved persona, content and boundaries, and you stay responsible for the account and its content.

Are the replies written by AI?

No. Every message your fans receive is sent by a human chatter, even when the model itself is AI-generated. We use AI inside our own tools to give chatters each fan’s context and keep your persona consistent across shifts. AI never chats with your fans.

Do you work with real Fanvue creators too?

Yes. We chat for real Fanvue creators as well as AI models. Either way you need content ready and a way of bringing fans in. We handle what happens in the DMs.

How do you get access to my Fanvue account?

We agree the right access during onboarding and check the scope with you before anyone starts chatting. You keep ownership of the account, and you don’t need to send any login details for the free estimate. How to add a chatter on Fanvue safely →

What does Fanvue chatting cost?

Usually 20–25% of the net tips and PPV our chatters close. No commission on subscriptions or renewals, no retainer, no minimum and no lock-in. Final terms are agreed before launch, and Fanvue’s own platform fee still applies. What Fanvue chatters really cost →

How do you keep my AI model’s voice consistent?

We turn your persona’s tone, backstory, boundaries and approved examples into one brief that every chatter works from. Fan notes carry over between shifts, so nobody has to ask a fan the same thing twice. Our Fanvue persona checklist →

How do you price PPV on Fanvue?

We build a pricing chart around your content and your fans, from teasers and bundles up to higher tiers for your biggest spenders. You approve the prices before we launch. Fanvue PPV pricing guide →

Do I still need to label AI content?

Yes. Human chatting doesn’t change Fanvue’s rules for AI-generated media, so keep following Fanvue’s AI content guidance. Fanvue’s AI content guidance ↗

How fast can we start?

Usually within 24 hours of your first message, once access and content are ready.

How often do you invoice?

Every two weeks. We total the period’s tips and PPV, apply the agreed percentage and send the invoice, payable by bank transfer or crypto.

Can I stop whenever I want?

Yes. No lock-in contracts, no notice periods and no exit fees.
