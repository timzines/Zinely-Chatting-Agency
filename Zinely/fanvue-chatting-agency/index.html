Human chatting for AI models with an audience

Zinely manages Fanvue DMs for AI-model operators and creators who already have traffic coming in. You build the persona, supply content and grow the audience. Our human team handles conversations, PPV offers, fan notes and follow-up.

This is a managed chatting service, not an AI-model generator or a traffic-acquisition package. If your character is ready but your inbox is empty, the first job is building a source of fans. If fans are arriving and you cannot cover the messages, we can review the account.

What do we need before taking over a Fanvue persona?

A useful handover includes an approved persona guide, a content library, offer boundaries and an account that is ready for managed access. A consistent brief gives each shift the same reference point.

  • Voice and continuity: character background, tone, established details and promises the team must not make.
  • Content and offers: what is available, what may be sold, and what needs your approval.
  • Audience: your traffic sources, new-fan flow and any existing purchase or conversation history.
  • Escalation: who decides when a fan asks for something outside the approved brief.

How should AI disclosure and account access work?

Fanvue’s AI-content guidance requires disclosure of AI-generated media. Human chatting does not change the fact that the persona or media is AI-generated. Keep the required disclosures in place.

Fanvue documents manager and team-member accounts for delegated work. Its guidance says managers cannot access payouts, change the account email or password, or delete the account. Review the actual permissions and the creators a team member can access before inviting anyone.

Yes — it's one of the things we're most often hired for. The persona can be AI-generated; the fans and their money are real. Our human chatters run the persona's DMs from a persona bible: consistent backstory, consistent voice, human judgment on pricing, timing and escalation.

Why use human chatters instead of an AI chatbot on Fanvue?

A human team provides managed coverage, account-specific judgment and a clear escalation path. Compare the service using your own account’s coverage, conversation quality and net revenue; we do not promise that any single approach wins on every account.
