You bring Zinely into your existing operation to manage creator DMs under your agency’s brand. Our team handles conversations, PPV offers, fan notes and shift coverage; your agency keeps the creator relationship and continues to drive traffic.

Is your agency ready to outsource?

The strongest fit is an agency with active creators, a repeatable source of traffic and content ready to sell. Outsourcing chatting solves an inbox and team-management problem; it does not create an audience for an empty account.

  • Fans are already arriving, but reply coverage or follow-up is inconsistent.
  • You spend time checking shifts, training replacements or reviewing conversations.
  • You can supply approved content and a clear voice guide for each creator.
  • You want a chatting partner while retaining responsibility for marketing.

There is no published minimum revenue requirement. Send the current account situation so we can assess fit. If you run your own account, see our OnlyFans chatting service for creators. For virtual creators, see human chatting for AI models on Fanvue.

How does the handover work?

Start with a review of your accounts, current coverage and content. Then agree the account-specific voice, boundaries, access and commercial terms before the team begins.

  1. Share the operating picture. Platforms, number of active creators, traffic sources, recent revenue mix and the gaps you want covered.
  2. Prepare each creator’s brief. Voice, approved offers, content library, previous fan notes and situations that need your decision.
  3. Agree access and responsibilities. Establish who can access each account, who approves changes and how access is removed when work ends. Use the platform’s supported access options.
  4. Confirm the launch. Our current onboarding target is usually within 24 hours of first contact; account readiness and agreed access determine the actual start.

How are multiple creators kept consistent?

Each creator needs a separate voice and set of boundaries. Fan notes let a new shift pick up a conversation without treating a returning buyer like a stranger. Team leaders review chats and handle escalations.

Before launch, agree who on your side owns approvals and how to raise an issue. Review results per account: combining a busy account with a quiet one can hide the coverage or conversion problem you need to fix.

Agency outsourcing questions

Is this a chatter recruitment service?

No. Zinely provides a managed chatting operation. We handle the team and shift coverage rather than supplying a list of candidates for you to employ.

Do we keep our creator relationships?

Yes. The service operates under your agency’s brand, while you retain the creator relationship and marketing responsibilities. Agree the working arrangement before access is granted.

Can you work with AI-model agencies?

Yes, for AI-model accounts on Fanvue. The account’s persona and content can be AI-generated; Zinely’s chatters are human. See the Fanvue handover requirements.

What should I include in my first message?

Say that you run an agency, name the platforms and number of active accounts, explain how you bring in traffic, and describe your current chatting setup. Share an approximate revenue range if useful; never send account passwords in the initial message.
