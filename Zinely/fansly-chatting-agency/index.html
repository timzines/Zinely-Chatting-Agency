Before we start

A few useful answers.

Have something specific to your account? Bring it to the first conversation.

Can you talk to free followers?

That depends on your Fansly message permissions. We review your settings before launch and agree how the team will work with followers and subscribers who can access the conversation.

How do you handle different tiers?

We review your tier benefits, media permissions and approved offers during onboarding. Chatters use that context, along with the fan’s purchase history, when discussing content.

Do you take a cut of subscriptions?

No. Zinely’s commission applies only to net tips and PPV our chatters close, at an agreed rate within our published 16.5–25% range. We do not commission subscriptions or renewals; platform fees still apply.

Do you bring traffic to my Fansly page?

This service covers chatting. You remain responsible for creating content and bringing in fans. It is best suited to accounts with an audience already arriving.
