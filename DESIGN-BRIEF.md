## 1. What the business is

**Zinely** is a white-label **chatting agency** for adult content creators and agencies on OnlyFans, Fanvue, Fansly, LoyalFans, and Fanfix. They supply trained, NDA-bound "chatters" who run a creator's DMs 24/7 (selling PPV, tips, customs, sexting, mass DMs, fan retention) under the creator's or agency's own brand. They charge a sliding performance rate (20–25% of gross) and lead with a free 5-day trial.

**Two audiences, one offer:**
- **Creators** — individual models who want their DMs run.
- **Agencies** — businesses with a roster of models who want to outsource the chatting layer invisibly under their own brand.

**Brand name:** Zinely
**Wordmark tagline:** "Chatting Agency"
**Descriptor line:** "Chatting partner for OnlyFans creators and agencies."
