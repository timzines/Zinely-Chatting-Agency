Before we start

A few useful answers.

Have something specific to your account? Bring it to the first conversation.

Do you chat for AI models on Fanvue?

Yes. We provide human chatting for Fanvue AI-model operators with existing traffic. We work from your approved persona, content and boundaries; you remain responsible for the account and its content.

Are the replies written by AI?

No. Zinely’s chatting service uses human chatters. AI-generated media or a fictional persona does not mean the conversation is handled by an AI chatbot.

Do you also work with real creators?

Yes. We support real Fanvue creators as well as AI-model accounts. Both need content ready and an existing way to attract fans.

How do we arrange account access?

We agree the appropriate access during onboarding and review the scope before chatting starts. You retain account ownership. There is no need to send credentials for the initial earnings estimate.
