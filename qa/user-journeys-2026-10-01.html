Trust and conversion changes

  • “Get a free earnings estimate” is the main action; compact navigation uses “Free estimate.” Telegram is identified as the channel.
  • The contact page explains the personal review, information to share, earnings range, assumptions and commission discussion.
  • The looping example purchase feed no longer claims to be live transactions.
  • The calculator no longer implies fixed numbers prove that every other agency or in-house team will earn less. Its assumptions and fee basis are visible.

Still worth resolving

  • Confirm whether agency commission tiers use each account’s revenue, combined roster revenue, or an individual agreement. The website does not invent an answer and asks prospects to discuss the applicable commission.
  • Test with real prospects next. In particular, ask whether the estimate offer feels specific enough and whether they understand the difference between managed chatting and full account management.
  • This review does not measure conversion rate or cover Safari/Firefox, real mobile hardware, load testing, or sending a lead through a logged-in Telegram app.
