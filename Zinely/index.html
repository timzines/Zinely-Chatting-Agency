Quick answers.

Will fans know it isn't me?

No. We onboard your tone, emoji habits, boundaries, and backstory, and chatters work from detailed fan notes. Human-only chatting — no AI, ever.

Do you take subscription money as well?

No — never. Subscriptions and renewals stay 100% yours. Our percentage applies only to the tips and PPVs our chatters close in the DMs.

How often do you get paid?

Once a week, every Monday. We total the week's tips & PPVs, apply the agreed percentage, and send the invoice — payable by bank transfer or crypto.

How do I know my account actually gets 24/7 chatting?

We have systems that monitor every account around the clock. If a chatter misses a shift, cover chatters step in — and if our systems detect no one active on your account, it's flagged to management immediately. Coverage isn't a promise, it's monitored.

Is my account safe with you?

Yes. Strict rules on vault content and customs, no improvising on sensitive situations — anything risky gets escalated to a team leader before anyone acts. Your account is treated like the business it is.

Can I stop whenever I want?

Yes — no lock-in contracts, no notice periods, no exit fees. We keep partners with results, not paperwork.

How fast can we start?

Usually within 48 hours of your message — onboarding captures your voice and content library, then our chatters go live.

I run an agency — can you take over our chatting?

Yes, that's most of our business. Your clients, your creators, our closers — and invisible to their fans.
