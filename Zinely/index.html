Quick answers.

Still curious? Talk to us ↗

Can I switch from my current chatting team?

Start with a free earnings estimate and tell us what isn’t working. We can discuss your current setup, voice, boundaries and handover before you decide whether to switch. You don’t need to share account access for that first review.

Do you work with AI models as well as real creators?

Yes. We manage chatting for real creators and AI-model operators, including on Fanvue. The persona can be AI-generated; our chatters are human. Your character guide, content boundaries and fan notes keep shifts consistent. See how Fanvue chatting works →

What do I get with the free earnings estimate?

A personal review from our team, an estimated earnings range with the assumptions explained, and the chatting opportunities we see. Send your platform, recent revenue, subscriber or traffic numbers, and what you want to improve on Telegram. For agencies, include the number of accounts. It’s free and there’s no commitment; an estimate is not a guarantee.

Will fans know it isn't me?

We build the chatting around your tone, emoji habits, boundaries and backstory. Detailed fan notes help each shift pick up naturally. Our chatters are human, including when the model is AI-generated.

Do you take subscription money as well?

No — never. Subscriptions and renewals stay 100% yours. Our percentage applies only to the tips and PPVs our chatters close in the DMs.

How often do you get paid?

Once a week, every Monday. We total the week's tips & PPVs, apply the agreed percentage, and send the invoice — payable by bank transfer or crypto.

How do I know my account actually gets 24/7 chatting?

We have systems that monitor every account around the clock. If a chatter misses a shift, cover chatters step in — and if our systems detect no one active on your account, it's flagged to management immediately. Coverage isn't a promise, it's monitored.

Is my account safe with you?

Yes. Strict rules on vault content and customs, no improvising on sensitive situations — anything risky gets escalated to a team leader before anyone acts. Your account is treated like the business it is.

Can I stop whenever I want?

Yes — no lock-in contracts, no notice periods, no exit fees. We keep partners with results, not paperwork.

How fast can we start?

Usually within 24 hours of your message — onboarding captures your voice and content library, then our chatters go live.

I run an agency — can you take over our chatting?

Yes, that's most of our business. Your clients, your creators, our closers — and invisible to their fans.
