What makes Fanvue different from OnlyFans

Fanvue runs on the same core loop as OnlyFans — subscriptions, pay-per-view messages, tips — but the platform has made two bets that change how the money works:

  • It welcomes AI creators. Per Fanvue's published terms, AI-generated creators are allowed on the platform with proper disclosure. That has pulled in a wave of AI models and virtual influencers that other platforms turn away.
  • It's a challenger platform. Fanvue is actively courting creators moving over from (or running alongside) OnlyFans, and its feature set — DMs, PPV, tips — is built to make the switch feel familiar to fans.

What hasn't changed: the inbox is still where the money is closed. A Fanvue subscription gets a fan in the door; the PPV unlocks and tips that decide whether the account actually earns happen one conversation at a time — same as on OnlyFans.

AI models still need human closers

Here's the paradox of the AI-creator boom: the persona can be generated, but the revenue can't be. Every dollar on a Fanvue account comes from a human fan having what feels like a private conversation. And that's exactly where automation falls apart:

  • Canned replies leak. In a long thread, chatbots forget the fan's job, contradict last week's story, and reuse lines. Casual fans shrug; the top spenders — the ones funding the account — notice and quietly leave.
  • Selling needs judgment. Knowing that a fan gets paid on Friday, is mid-breakup, or hates being rushed isn't in a prompt. It's in fan notes kept by a person who reads the room before naming a price.
  • The chat is the product. For an AI model there's no "real her" behind the content — the DM relationship is the entire experience a fan is paying for. Handing that to a script is handing away the product itself.

Our position is simple: AI persona, human closers. Zinely chatters run the character from a persona bible — one consistent backstory, voice, and set of boundaries — with a real person making every pricing and timing call. Never AI-generated replies, on any account.
