Pricing questions.

Why percentage instead of a flat fee?

Because it puts us on your side of the table. A flat fee gets paid whether your DMs earn or not — a percentage only pays us when you get paid. Our incentive is your revenue, full stop.

How does the sliding rate work?

For OnlyFans and Fansly, the rate is tied to your monthly revenue: 25% up to $40k, 20% from $40k to $90k, and 16.5% above $90k. Fanvue commission is usually 20–25% of net tips and PPV our chatters close. Final terms are agreed before launch.

Are there any other costs?

No. No setup fee, no retainer, no minimum, no per-chatter charge. The percentage is the whole price.

How does billing work?

Every two weeks we total the period's tips & PPVs, take our percentage, and send you the invoice — payable by bank transfer or crypto. We agree the full payment details with you before we start.

Can I leave whenever I want?

Yes. No lock-in contracts, no notice period, no exit fee. We remove our access the day you stop. Read our working terms →

Is there a trial?

Yes. The trial week is seven days of 24/7 chatting, paid by commission at your normal rate, with no commitment: if you don't like us after day 7, you don't proceed. It's for accounts averaging 9–10 or more new free subscribers a day. How the trial week works →
