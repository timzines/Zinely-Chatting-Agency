# Zinely SEO and distribution strategy — October 2026

The objective is qualified conversations with agency owners, established OnlyFans creators, and Fanvue AI-model operators. The primary success measure is qualified enquiries and new client revenue attributable to organic discovery. Raw visits, article count and rank screenshots are supporting measures.

This replaces the priority order in the supplied July plan. Existing platform pages and the blog are useful foundations. The missing piece was a distinct buyer journey for agency owners, and a clear traffic-readiness requirement for creators.

## Changes to the July assumptions

- Daily work is useful; daily article publishing is not a requirement. Start with at most two thoroughly reviewed articles a week, plus updates to existing pages. Research and operational examples must add something beyond a generic AI answer. Google warns that generating many pages without value may constitute scaled content abuse ([Google guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/using-gen-ai-content)).
- No fixed word count or 40–50% uniqueness quota. A genuinely different audience problem determines whether a page deserves to exist. Google identifies no ideal length and emphasizes useful, distinctive content ([Google's AI-search guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide)).
- Keep helpful on-page FAQs, but remove FAQ rich results as a KPI. Google records the feature's withdrawal from Search starting May 7, 2026 ([documentation update](https://developers.google.com/search/updates?hl=en)). Existing valid FAQ markup is not an emergency to remove; it is not a priority deliverable.
- `llms.txt` is optional documentation, not an indexing or ranking strategy. Google says it ignores these files for Search. Do not spend the first month maintaining a second copy of all pricing facts ([Google guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide)).
- Search access and model-training access are separate decisions. Do not blanket-allow all AI bots as an SEO requirement. Check actual crawler access and current official bot documentation; allowlisting in robots does not override WAF rules. No robots changes were needed for the local wildcard allow policy.
- Crawler Hints uses cache signals and can notify IndexNow participants; it is not a guaranteed deployment webhook or a guarantee of indexing ([Cloudflare documentation](https://developers.cloudflare.com/cache/advanced-configuration/crawler-hints/)).
- Do not assume low competition, platform earnings superiority, rankings, instant AI citations or that Bing indexing guarantees a ChatGPT recommendation. Record evidence and uncertainty. A manual assistant query is a sampled visibility observation, not a market-share metric.
- Use the current site's 24-hour onboarding target, not the old plan's 48 hours. Present it as typical and conditional on account readiness. Confirm terms before expanding the claim. Monday is weekly invoicing, not a promise that a platform pays creators on Monday.
- Avoid unsupported promises that fans never notice, any account is completely safe, chatbots always fail, a particular DM ratio predicts success, or replying faster fixes most churn. State what Zinely actually does and what the evidence supports.
- Separate cumulative earnings from monthly revenue and the historical case-study rank from today's rank. An OnlyFans case study is not proof of Fanvue outcomes. Add dates and denominators wherever numerical proof appears.

## Daily work and weekly focus

Every day: check site health and pending work, inspect available performance evidence, research one relevant buyer question, then complete one bounded improvement or a verified content brief. Record what changed and why. If an approval or data connection is pending, use the time for useful research without creating an ever-growing pile of nearly identical drafts.

| Day | Focus |
|---|---|
| Monday | Query/page performance and the highest-impact existing-page improvement |
| Tuesday | One commercial article draft with a concrete example or checklist |
| Wednesday | Internal links, evidence, metadata and conversion-path review |
| Thursday | One second article draft only if the queue has capacity |
| Friday | Distribution research and a weekly scorecard; prepare a small number of relevant placements or community answers |
| Saturday | Current-source checks, case-study labels, stale/broken content |
| Sunday | Queue cleanup, next-week priorities and site health; no filler article |

The exact daily procedure and limits are in [runbook.md](runbook.md).

## Distribution that reaches the right people

SEO is the durable acquisition channel; relevant distribution helps the right buyers encounter the work sooner. Research active creator-operations communities, agency communities, CRM/integration partner resources, and Fanvue operator resources. Verify each destination's current rules and that it actually reaches one of the three audiences. Build a list with source URL, audience fit, rules, editorial contact path and the specific useful contribution.

Draft helpful answers to real questions, with affiliation disclosed if used. Propose a practical handover checklist or a clearly documented case study to relevant editors and partners. Do not buy links, manufacture reviews, create fake recommendations, submit bulk directory listings, or post the same pitch everywhere. Outreach and community posting need the user's explicit instruction identifying the channel and scope. This routine prepares opportunities and copy; it sends nothing.

## Measurement

Record the landing page and audience (agency / creator / Fanvue operator) for every enquiry where available. A Telegram click is an expression of interest, not a completed lead. The website cannot see whether a Telegram conversation became a customer without an agreed CRM/manual reconciliation process.

Recommended funnel: search impression → organic visit → estimate/contact/Telegram click → actual enquiry → qualified enquiry → signed client. Define a qualified enquiry as a matching decision-maker with active accounts, traffic or an acquisition process, and a need for chatting. Do not fabricate a numerical minimum revenue requirement.

Track weekly: non-brand search impressions and clicks per target page, queries by buyer intent, CTA clicks, actual enquiries, qualified enquiries, wins and revenue. Segment countries when sufficient data exists. Compare 28 complete days with the prior 28; annotate publication, major traffic and content changes. Search data can arrive late and low counts are noisy. Missing access means “unavailable,” not zero. Set numerical improvement targets after the baseline exists.

CTA measurement should capture page path, audience and CTA label through the selected analytics provider. Avoid names, fan data, account passwords and message contents in analytics. A CRM should record referral source with permission. Define and validate this before reporting conversion rates. Do not install an arbitrary provider or pretend an unconnected event listener is measurement.

## Codex and dots

Use one scheduled owner. Codex is practical here because this task already has the repository and can produce concrete edits. A daily task can run checks, research and prepare drafts, with useful findings reported in this task. Local scheduled work requires the computer to remain on and the app running ([scheduled-task documentation](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/automations)).

If “dots” means ChatGPT dots, it can own the same recurring brief through its scheduled-task workflow ([dot controls](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/dots/controls)). Give it accessible source files and the runbook; verify its repository/app access first. Do not run two independent agents publishing the same queue. No dot was configured in this implementation.

An always-on cloud workflow is a later option: repository access, a scheduled runner, current-source research, a review branch/PR, deterministic checks and the existing Cloudflare deployment. The current checkout has no recorded deployment configuration, so the routine must not invent one. Avoid adding an extra paid automation service before a simple daily task is producing useful work.
