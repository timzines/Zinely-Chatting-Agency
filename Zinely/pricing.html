Pricing questions.

Why percentage instead of a flat fee?

Because it puts us on your side of the table. A flat fee gets paid whether your DMs earn or not — a percentage only pays us when you get paid. Our incentive is your revenue, full stop.

How does the sliding rate work?

The rate is tied to your monthly revenue: 25% up to $40k, 20% from $40k to $90k, and 16.5% from $90k to $140k. As you grow, you automatically keep a bigger share — it's all agreed in writing before we start.

Are there any other costs?

No. No setup fee, no retainer, no minimum, no per-chatter charge. The percentage is the whole price.

How does billing work?

Every Monday we total the week's tips & PPVs, take our percentage, and send you the amount — payable by bank transfer or crypto. The full details are in the onboarding doc (PDF ↓).

Can I leave whenever I want?

Yes. No lock-in contracts. We keep partners with results, not paperwork.
