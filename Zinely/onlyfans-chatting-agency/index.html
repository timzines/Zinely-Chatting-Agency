Before we start

A few useful answers.

Have something specific to your account? Bring it to the first conversation.

Do I need an existing audience?

Yes. This service is for creators with fans arriving and content ready to sell. You keep responsibility for traffic and promotion; chatting alone cannot create that demand.

Can you work with my agency?

Yes. Our white label service lets you keep the creator relationship and marketing while Zinely handles chatting. We agree the workflow and account responsibilities with you before launch.

Will the chatters sound like me?

We learn from your approved examples, tone and boundaries. Fan notes help the next shift continue the conversation, and we bring questions outside the brief back to you.

What do you charge commission on?

Our published range is 16.5–25% of the net tips and PPV our chatters close. We do not commission subscriptions or renewals. Platform fees still apply, and final terms are agreed before launch.
